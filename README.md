Project Overview
This repository features a comprehensive Emergency Room Performance Dashboard designed to optimize hospital emergency room operations. The project includes detailed metrics, interactive visuals, and actionable insights, showcasing my expertise in data analysis, Power BI, and creating impactful dashboards for decision-making.

Business Objectives
The main objectives of the project are:

Streamline operations in hospital emergency rooms by providing data-driven insights.
Track critical performance indicators such as wait times, patient satisfaction, and demographics.
Enable stakeholders to make informed decisions regarding resource allocation and service optimization.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard tracks the following KPIs:

Number of Patients: Total daily, monthly, and aggregated patient visits.
Average Wait Time: Average duration before a patient receives medical attention.
Patient Satisfaction Score: Measuring service quality (scale of 1–10).
Referral Statistics: Tracking department referrals and identifying high-demand areas.
Patient Demographics: Insights into age, gender, and racial distributions.
Data Preparation
I undertook the following steps to ensure high-quality data for analysis:

Data Cleaning:
Removed duplicates, handled missing values, and resolved outliers.
Standardized fields like patient demographics and admission details.
Integration:
Merged multiple datasets (e.g., patient data, date data) for cohesive analysis.
Custom Calculations:
Created calculated fields for wait times, satisfaction rates, and performance metrics.
Date Table:
Prepared a cleaned date table to facilitate accurate time-based analysis.
Dashboard Features
1. Monthly View
This dashboard provides a monthly analysis of key metrics. Features include:

Patient Admission Status: Breakdown of admitted vs. non-admitted patients.
Wait Time Analysis: Percentage of patients seen within the target time of 30 minutes.
Demographics: Age, gender, and race distributions.
Department Referrals: Trends in referrals to various departments.
Hourly Trends: Patient volumes by day and hour, highlighting peak periods.
2. Consolidated View
Offers a holistic summary of all metrics across a customizable date range. Highlights include:

Aggregated metrics for patient volume, satisfaction, and admission status.
Department referral trends across the entire dataset.
Visualizations of peak hours, age group distributions, and demographic insights.
3. Patient Details
Provides granular, patient-level insights. Key attributes include:

Patient ID, name, gender, age, and admission date.
Department referral, wait time, and admission status for each patient.
4. Key Takeaways
Summarizes the dashboard's findings, including:

Performance Insights: Trends in patient wait times, satisfaction, and department referrals.
Demographic Patterns: Age, race, and gender distribution.
Operational Bottlenecks: Identified peak busy periods and staffing challenges.
Actionable Recommendations: Insights to improve resource allocation and patient care.
